Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Overview

The Offline Knowledge Compendium is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data in our progressive web application. Because the Offline Knowledge Compendium operates as an offline-first PWA with no backend database at runtime, your privacy is protected through our architecture: all data collection is local to your device.

2. What Data We Collect

The Offline Knowledge Compendium collects minimal data, and only on your device through browser localStorage. Specifically, we store:

Reading Progress: Which guides you have marked as read or completed. This helps the app remember your advancement through the material.

Bookmarks: Sections within guides that you have bookmarked for quick reference. These are stored locally so you can access your saved sections across sessions.

Personal Notes: Any notes you write while reading guides. These notes are stored entirely on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Theme Preference: Your choice of dark or light theme is saved locally to maintain your preference when you return to the app.

3. No Personal Data Collection

The Offline Knowledge Compendium does not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or any personally identifiable information. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any credentials. All data remains on your device and is not sent to our servers or any third parties (except as noted below).

4. How We Store Your Data

All the data described in Section 2 is stored in your browser's localStorage. This is a local storage mechanism on your device—data does not leave your device unless you explicitly download or export it. When you clear your browser's cache or storage, all app data is removed.

5. Third-Party Services

The Offline Knowledge Compendium may integrate third-party advertising or analytics services to support ongoing development and operations. If we use services such as Google AdMob or similar advertising networks, those services may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies, including device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android), general location information (country/region level), and usage analytics. We do not control how these third-party services use data, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies directly. You may be able to opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored by the Offline Knowledge Compendium remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete all app data at any time by clearing your browser's storage or uninstalling the app. If you wish to remove only your personal notes or bookmarks, you can manage these through the app interface.

7. Children Under 13 (COPPA Compliance)

The Offline Knowledge Compendium is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app without parental consent.

8. GDPR and International Privacy

For users in the European Union and other jurisdictions with privacy regulations: Because all Offline Knowledge Compendium data is processed locally on your device and never transmitted to servers, we have no user-level databases to access, modify, or delete on our end. Your data is entirely under your control. If you wish to ensure all data is deleted, clear your browser storage or uninstall the app. We do not process personal data on centralized servers, which means GDPR data subject rights (access, correction, deletion requests) are not applicable in the traditional sense—you have direct control over your own data on your device.

9. Data Security

Because your data remains on your device and is not transmitted over the internet by the Offline Knowledge Compendium itself, the security of your data depends on your device's security. We recommend keeping your device and browser up to date with the latest security patches. We do not use encryption for localStorage (as it is already isolated per-origin by the browser), but data transmission is not involved in our architecture.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance reasons. Updates will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the app after changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
