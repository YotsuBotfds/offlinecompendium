CPR Quick Reference

Adult

30
Compressions
2
Breaths

Depth: 2 inches Rate: 100-120/min

Place heel of hand on center of chest

Child

Same 30:2 ratio Depth: 1.5 inches

Infant

2 fingers, 1.5 inches deep Same 30:2 ratio

Call emergency services immediately

Choking Response

Conscious Adult

5
Back Blows
5
Abdominal Thrusts

Repeat until object dislodges or person collapses

Abdominal Thrust (Heimlich)

Stand behind victim Place fist above navel, below ribcage Grasp fist with other hand Quick upward thrust

Unconscious Victim

Begin CPR Check mouth before each rescue breath Remove object if visible

Infant (Under 1 Year)

5 back blows (face-down) 5 chest thrusts (2 fingers) Check mouth, repeat

Severe Bleeding Control

Step 1: Direct Pressure

Apply pressure with clean cloth

Hold for 10-15 minutes DO NOT remove cloth, just add more on top

Step 2: Elevation

Raise wounded area above heart

Step 3: Tourniquet (Last Resort)

Use ONLY if bleeding won't stop

Place 2 inches above wound Tighten until bleeding stops WRITE TIME on tourniquet marker

NEVER remove tourniquet

Get emergency medical help NOW

Pressure Points

Inner arm (brachial artery) Inner groin (femoral artery) Neck side (carotid artery)

ORS (Oral Rehydration)

Life-Saving Formula

1 Liter Clean Water

6 Teaspoons Sugar

½ Teaspoon Salt

Instructions

Mix ingredients thoroughly Sip slowly and continuously Small, frequent amounts work best

Use For

Severe diarrhea Vomiting Dehydration Heat exhaustion

Critical Note

CAN SAVE LIVES in severe dehydration

Start rehydration immediately when symptoms appear Continue for 24-48 hours minimum

Fire Starting Checklist

Layer 1: Tinder

Dry, fingernail-sized pieces

Dry leaves, bark, paper Must be very fine and dry

Layer 2: Kindling

Pencil-thick sticks

Arrange in teepee shape Leave center open for tinder

Layer 3: Fuel

Wrist-thick logs

Add after kindling catches

Critical Setup

Shield from wind Ground underneath must be clear Water source nearby

Ignition

Light tinder center Blow gently at base to spread flame Add kindling as tinder burns

Shelter Priorities

Priority Order

  1. Get OFF ground
  2. Block WIND
  3. INSULATE from cold

Key Principle

SMALLER space = WARMER shelter

Don't make it too large Just big enough to lie down

Debris Hut

Lean sticks against ridgepole Pile leaves 3 feet thick Cover floor with insulation Create small entrance only

Emergency Materials

Leaves, pine needles, bark Moss, grass, clothing Anything between you and ground

Rule of 3s

Survival priority sequence:

3 Minutes without air/oxygen

3 Hours without shelter
(in extreme weather)

3 Days without water

3 Weeks without food

What This Means

AIR is the absolute first priority SHELTER before water in extreme conditions WATER before food (longer survival window) FOOD has the longest window

Manage priorities in order of urgency

Universal Edibility Test

For unknown plants only. High failure risk.

  1. Separate plant parts (roots, stems, leaves)
  2. Smell — reject if foul
  3. Skin contact test 15 min
  4. Lip contact test 15 min
  5. Tongue contact test 15 min
  6. Chew, spit, wait 15 min
  7. Swallow small amount, wait 8 hours
  8. Eat larger amount, wait 24 hours

If ANY symptom appears, induce vomiting and seek help

Danger Signs

Nausea, cramping, diarrhea Itching lips or mouth Burning sensation Milky sap or umbrella-shaped flowers (avoid)

Essential Knots

1. Bowline

Creates fixed loop; won't slip. Perfect for rescue.

2. Clove Hitch

Quick tie to post or tree; easy to untie.

3. Taut-Line Hitch

Adjustable loop that holds under tension.

4. Square Knot

Join two ropes of equal diameter. Right over left.

5. Figure-8 Knot

Stopper knot at end of rope. Won't slip through.

6. Trucker's Hitch

Mechanical advantage knot for heavy loads.

Burns Treatment

1st Degree (Red, No Blistering)

Cool with water 10-20 min

Apply aloe vera gel Pain relief as needed

2nd Degree (Blistered, Red)

Cool with water immediately

Do NOT pop blisters Cover loosely with clean cloth Antibiotic ointment optional

3rd Degree (Charred, White, Deep)

DO NOT apply water to large areas

Cover with CLEAN cloth only

Treat for shock (lay flat, elevate legs) Emergency medical help URGENT

All Burns

Remove tight jewelry and clothing

Monitor for airway obstruction Watch for signs of shock

Seek medical help for burns larger than 3 inches
