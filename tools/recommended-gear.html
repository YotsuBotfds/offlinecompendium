⚡ Recommended Gear

Power independence gear organized by urgency. Every recommendation links to the guide that explains how to use it. Every category includes a no-purchase alternative.

Our approach: Honest pros and cons for every item. No gear is perfect — we tell you the trade-offs. These are genuinely life-critical recommendations, not filler affiliate content.

🔴 Tier 1 — Buy Now While You Can

Pre-disaster preparation. These are the items to acquire while supply chains still work.

☀️

Solar Power Bank

Portable battery with built-in solar panel. Your first line of defense against dead devices.

20,000+ mAh Built-in panel USB-A + USB-C
✓ No setup needed — charge and go
✓ Charges phone 4-5x from stored battery
✗ Built-in panel is slow (8-20 hrs to self-charge)
✗ Panel degrades after 3-5 years
No-purchase alternative: Use any vehicle's 12V port with a USB adapter (see Emergency Power Bootstrap guide)
📖 Emergency Power Bootstrap
🔋

Foldable Solar Panel (20W+)

Lightweight, packable panel that charges devices directly via USB in sunlight.

20-100W Foldable Direct USB output
✓ Best watt-per-dollar and watt-per-pound ratio
✓ Phone charges in 2-3 hrs direct sun
✗ No built-in battery — only works in sunlight
✗ Output drops significantly in clouds/shade
No-purchase alternative: Salvage panels from garden lights, road signs, or rooftop installations (see Solar Technology guide)
📖 Solar Technology
📻

Hand-Crank Emergency Radio

AM/FM/NOAA radio with hand crank, solar panel, flashlight, and USB charging port.

Multi-band radio Hand crank USB out
✓ Three power sources: crank, solar, USB-in
✓ Receives emergency broadcasts (NOAA)
✗ Slow phone charging (~15 min cranking = 5 min talk)
✗ Internal battery loses capacity over years
No-purchase alternative: Build a bicycle generator for much higher output (see Power Generation guide)
📖 Power Generation
🔌

Portable Battery Station

Large-capacity battery with AC outlets, USB ports, and solar input. Powers laptops, lights, and tools.

300-2000 Wh AC + DC + USB Solar input
✓ Powers laptops and small appliances
✓ 500Wh unit = 40+ phone charges
✗ Heavy (10-50 lbs depending on capacity)
✗ Expensive ($200-$2000+)
No-purchase alternative: Recondition a car battery + inverter from any vehicle (see Battery Restoration guide)

🟡 Tier 2 — Long-Term Power Independence

Infrastructure for sustainable off-grid electricity. Invest in these for permanent installations.

⚡

Rigid Solar Panel Kit + Charge Controller

Permanent-mount solar panels with MPPT charge controller for efficient battery charging.

100-400W per panel 25-year lifespan MPPT controller
✓ Most reliable long-term power source
✓ No moving parts — minimal maintenance
✗ Requires mounting infrastructure
✗ Needs charge controller + batteries for storage
No-purchase alternative: Salvage panels from buildings, construction sites, or solar farms. Clean and test — most survive intact.
📖 Solar Technology
🔋

Deep-Cycle Batteries (LiFePO4)

Lithium iron phosphate batteries for solar energy storage. Safe chemistry, 10+ year lifespan.

100-200 Ah 12V or 48V 3000+ cycles
✓ 10x longer lifespan than lead-acid
✓ No maintenance, no acid, no gas venting
✗ Higher upfront cost than lead-acid
✗ Cannot be recharged below freezing
No-purchase alternative: Recondition salvaged lead-acid batteries from vehicles, UPS units, and alarm systems (see Battery Restoration guide)
📖 Battery Restoration
🌬️

Small Wind Turbine

400-1000W wind generator for locations with consistent wind. Complements solar for 24/7 generation.

400-1000W 12/24V DC 10-25 mph wind
✓ Generates power at night and in storms
✓ Pairs with solar for reliable 24/7 power
✗ Needs consistent wind (coastal, hilltops, plains)
✗ Moving parts require maintenance
No-purchase alternative: Build a turbine from a car alternator + PVC pipe blades (see Power Generation guide)
📖 Power Generation
💧

Micro-Hydro Kit

Small water turbine for continuous power generation from streams or rivers with sufficient flow and drop.

100-1000W Needs 1m+ head 24/7 output
✓ Most consistent renewable — runs 24/7/365
✓ Higher capacity factor than solar or wind
✗ Requires flowing water with elevation drop
✗ Intake screens need regular clearing
No-purchase alternative: Build from a salvaged motor + improvised turbine wheel (see Hydroelectric Power guide)
📖 Hydroelectric Power
