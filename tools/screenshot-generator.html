How to Use

  1. Select Platform: Choose between Google Play (1080x1920) or Apple App Store (1290x2796) dimensions.
  2. Preview: The screenshots will display in the selected format. Each one shows a different feature of the Offline Knowledge Compendium app.
  3. Download All: Click the "Download All Screenshots" button to export all 6 screenshots as PNG images. They will be saved to your Downloads folder with names like compendium-screenshot-1.png, compendium-screenshot-2.png, etc.
  4. Individual Export: Each screenshot can be downloaded individually by right-clicking the phone frame and selecting "Save image as".
  5. Dimensions: The exported images are full resolution and ready for upload to Google Play Console or Apple App Store Connect.
  6. Customization: Edit the content inside the phone frames by modifying the HTML directly if needed.

Note: Screenshots are generated using html2canvas and may look slightly different when printed vs. on screen due to rendering differences. Preview before uploading to app stores.
